About Us

Luxport Trading Limited is a Hong Kong–based trading company specializing in the global distribution of watches, perfumes, designer bags, and sunglasses.

We connect trusted suppliers and retailers across international markets, delivering authentic, high-quality products that reflect timeless style and craftsmanship.

At Luxport, our goal is simple — to make luxury accessible and reliable.

Our Approach

Through a strong global sourcing network, strategic partnerships, and deep market insight, we ensure that every product we trade meets the highest standards of authenticity and excellence.

With Hong Kong's reputation as a leading international trade hub, Luxport Trading operates at the crossroads of East and West — enabling fast logistics, competitive pricing, and unmatched customer service for our global clients.

Our Mission

To be a trusted global partner in the goods trade, providing premium products and dependable service with integrity and efficiency.

Our Vision

To become a leading name in international luxury trading, recognized for authenticity, trust, and long-term partnerships.
